Publication and Research Direction

Paper · SN Computer Science

A Neurosymbolic Multimodal Framework for 30-Day Diabetic Readmission Prediction: Integrating Structured EHR Data, Dense Clinical Embeddings, and Knowledge-Grounded Symbolic Extraction

Una Suman Kumar Patro, Sibangi Subhadarsani, Narendra Badam. I am co-author, contributing on predictive modelling and the pipeline.

Under review

My research direction grew out of industry work and one simple question I kept asking in supermarkets and watching Amazon and Costco: how do they hold enough stock to deliver on time without waste? Three linked threads in management analytics and supply chain.

1

Delivery-date and shipping-cost prediction

Forecasting when an order will really arrive, and what it will cost, building directly on the ATP delivery-date work at Johnson & Johnson.

2

Inventory optimisation

Holding stock so there is neither overstock nor a failed delivery from low stock, with cost kept in view at every step.

3

Perishable, expiry-aware inventory

Food, packaged food, clothing, and other supermarket goods wasted through over-supply, expiry, a trend ending, or a lifestyle change. Managing that stock so people get what they need on time, with less waste.
